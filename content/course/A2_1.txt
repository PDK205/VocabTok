## grammar
title: Quá khứ của to be: was / were
summary: Was / were là dạng quá khứ của am / is / are, dùng để nói về trạng thái, vị trí, cảm xúc trong quá khứ: I was tired yesterday.
formula: I / He / She / It + was
formula: You / We / They + were
formula: Phủ định: wasn't (was not) / weren't (were not)
formula: Câu hỏi: Was / Were + S + ...? → Yes, I was. / No, they weren't.
point: Dấu hiệu thời gian | yesterday, last night / week / year, two days ago, in 2020, when I was a child.
point: There was / There were | Quá khứ của there is / there are: There was a big storm. / There were many tourists.
point: Hỏi về kỳ nghỉ | How was your holiday? – It was great! / Where were you last weekend? – I was in Da Lat.
example: I was at the beach yesterday. | Hôm qua tôi ở bãi biển.
example: The hotel was clean and cheap. | Khách sạn sạch sẽ và rẻ.
example: We weren't at home last weekend. | Cuối tuần trước chúng tôi không ở nhà.
example: Was the weather nice? Yes, it was. | Thời tiết có đẹp không? Có.
mistake: Yesterday I am very tired. | Yesterday I was very tired. | yesterday → dùng quá khứ.
mistake: They was happy. | They were happy. | They đi với were.
ex: I ___ in Nha Trang last summer. | was ; were ; am | was | I → was; last summer → quá khứ.
ex: My parents ___ at home yesterday. | was ; were ; are | were | My parents = they → were.
ex: ___ the hotel expensive? | Was ; Were ; Did | Was | the hotel số ít → Was.
ex: There ___ many people on the beach. | was ; were ; is | were | many people số nhiều → were.
ex: We ___ very tired last night. | was ; were ; are | were | We → were.
ex: It ___ sunny yesterday. It was rainy. | wasn't ; weren't ; isn't | wasn't | It → wasn't.
ex: How ___ your holiday? It was great! | was ; were ; did | was | your holiday số ít → was.

## grammar
title: Thì quá khứ đơn (Past Simple)
summary: Thì quá khứ đơn diễn tả hành động đã xảy ra và kết thúc tại một thời điểm xác định trong quá khứ. Động từ có quy tắc thêm -ed; động từ bất quy tắc phải học thuộc (xem bảng Động từ bất quy tắc trong mục Tài liệu).
formula: Khẳng định: S + V-ed / V2: I visited, she went
formula: Phủ định: S + didn't + V nguyên mẫu: He didn't go.
formula: Câu hỏi: Did + S + V nguyên mẫu? → Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
formula: Có từ để hỏi: Wh- + did + S + V?: Where did you go?
point: Quy tắc thêm -ed | Thường thêm -ed (walk → walked); tận cùng e chỉ thêm d (live → lived); phụ âm + y → ied (study → studied); nhân đôi phụ âm cuối (stop → stopped).
point: Cách đọc đuôi -ed | /t/ sau âm vô thanh p, k, f, s, ʃ, tʃ (stopped, watched); /ɪd/ sau t, d (wanted, needed); /d/ trong các trường hợp còn lại (played, cleaned).
point: Động từ bất quy tắc thường gặp | go → went, have → had, see → saw, buy → bought, eat → ate, leave → left, lose → lost, find → found, forget → forgot, take → took.
point: Có did thì động từ về nguyên mẫu | Did you went? (sai) → Did you go? (đúng). She didn't came (sai) → She didn't come.
example: I lost my keys yesterday. | Hôm qua tôi làm mất chìa khóa.
example: We arrived at the airport two hours ago. | Chúng tôi đến sân bay hai tiếng trước.
example: She didn't remember my name. | Cô ấy không nhớ tên tôi.
example: What did you do last weekend? | Cuối tuần trước bạn đã làm gì?
mistake: I goed to school. | I went to school. | go là động từ bất quy tắc.
mistake: He didn't came. | He didn't come. | Sau didn't dùng V nguyên mẫu.
mistake: Where you went? | Where did you go? | Câu hỏi cần trợ động từ did.
ex: We ___ a great film last night. | see ; saw ; seen | saw | see – saw – seen; quá khứ đơn dùng V2.
ex: She ___ her homework yesterday. | finish ; finished ; finishs | finished | Động từ có quy tắc thêm -ed.
ex: I ___ my phone at the café. | lose ; losed ; lost | lost | lose – lost – lost.
ex: ___ you call your mother yesterday? | Do ; Did ; Were | Did | Câu hỏi quá khứ đơn dùng Did.
ex: They didn't ___ the bus. | catch ; caught ; catched | catch | Sau didn't dùng V nguyên mẫu.
ex: When ___ he arrive? | did ; was ; does | did | Wh- + did + S + V.
ex: My grandfather ___ in a small village when he was young. | lives ; lived ; living | lived | when he was young → quá khứ.

## grammar
title: Should / Shouldn't (lời khuyên)
summary: Should dùng để đưa ra lời khuyên hoặc nói điều nên làm; shouldn't là điều không nên làm. Giống can, should không chia theo chủ ngữ và luôn đi với động từ nguyên mẫu.
formula: S + should + V nguyên mẫu: You should rest.
formula: S + shouldn't + V nguyên mẫu: You shouldn't smoke.
formula: Câu hỏi: Should + S + V?: Should I see a doctor?
formula: Xin lời khuyên: What should I do?
point: Khuyên nhẹ nhàng | I think you should... / Maybe you should... / I don't think you should... giúp câu khuyên mềm mỏng, lịch sự hơn.
point: Không thêm to sau should | You should to go (sai) → You should go (đúng). Khác với ought to (cũng nghĩa là nên) phải có to.
point: Các cách khuyên khác | Why don't you + V? (Sao bạn không...?) / If I were you, I'd + V (Nếu tôi là bạn, tôi sẽ...).
example: You should drink more water. | Bạn nên uống nhiều nước hơn.
example: You shouldn't eat too much sugar. | Bạn không nên ăn quá nhiều đường.
example: Should I take this medicine before meals? | Tôi có nên uống thuốc này trước bữa ăn không?
example: I think you should go to bed early. | Tôi nghĩ bạn nên đi ngủ sớm.
mistake: You should to rest. | You should rest. | Sau should không có to.
mistake: She shoulds see a doctor. | She should see a doctor. | should không chia theo chủ ngữ.
ex: You have a fever. You ___ stay at home. | should ; shouldn't ; can't | should | Bị sốt → nên ở nhà.
ex: You ___ drink coffee before bed. | should ; shouldn't ; must | shouldn't | Cà phê trước khi ngủ → không nên.
ex: He should ___ more vegetables. | eat ; eats ; to eat | eat | Sau should dùng V nguyên mẫu.
ex: ___ I call a doctor? | Do ; Should ; Am | Should | Xin lời khuyên: Should I...?
ex: My throat hurts. What ___ I do? | should ; do ; am | should | What should I do?
ex: Why don't you ___ some rest? | take ; taking ; to take | take | Why don't you + V nguyên mẫu.
ex: You shouldn't ___ so many sweets. | eat ; eating ; ate | eat | Sau shouldn't dùng V nguyên mẫu.

## grammar
title: So sánh hơn & so sánh nhất
summary: So sánh hơn dùng khi so sánh HAI người / vật; so sánh nhất dùng khi so sánh từ BA trở lên. Cách tạo phụ thuộc vào độ dài của tính từ.
formula: Tính từ ngắn: adj-er + than (cheaper than) · the + adj-est (the cheapest)
formula: Tính từ dài: more + adj + than (more modern than) · the most + adj (the most modern)
formula: Bất quy tắc: good → better → the best · bad → worse → the worst · far → farther → the farthest
point: Tính từ ngắn và dài | Tính từ ngắn: 1 âm tiết (cheap, big) và 2 âm tiết tận cùng -y (busy → busier, noisy → the noisiest). Tính từ dài: các tính từ 2 âm tiết trở lên còn lại (modern, crowded, peaceful).
point: Quy tắc chính tả | Tận cùng e: thêm r / st (nice → nicer). Phụ âm–nguyên âm–phụ âm: nhân đôi (big → bigger, hot → hottest). -y → -ier / -iest (happy → happier).
point: So sánh bằng | as + adj + as: The village is as beautiful as a painting. Phủ định: not as + adj + as (= kém hơn).
point: Nhấn mạnh | much / a lot / far + so sánh hơn: much bigger, a lot more expensive.
example: The city is noisier than the countryside. | Thành phố ồn hơn nông thôn.
