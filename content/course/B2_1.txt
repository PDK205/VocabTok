## grammar
title: Tương lai tiếp diễn & Tương lai hoàn thành
summary: Tương lai tiếp diễn (will be + V-ing) diễn tả hành động đang diễn ra tại một thời điểm trong tương lai. Tương lai hoàn thành (will have + V3) diễn tả hành động sẽ hoàn tất trước một thời điểm trong tương lai.
formula: Tương lai tiếp diễn: S + will be + V-ing (This time tomorrow, I'll be flying to Tokyo.)
formula: Tương lai hoàn thành: S + will have + V3 (By 2030, robots will have replaced many jobs.)
formula: Dấu hiệu: this time next week, at 8 p.m. tomorrow · by + mốc thời gian, by the time + S + V (hiện tại đơn)
point: Đang diễn ra tại thời điểm tương lai | At 9 a.m. tomorrow, I'll be presenting our new app. (Đúng 9 giờ sáng mai, tôi đang thuyết trình.)
point: Hoàn tất trước một mốc | By the end of this year, we will have launched three products. by = trước / không muộn hơn.
point: Hỏi lịch sự về kế hoạch | Will you be using the car tonight? (hỏi nhẹ nhàng về kế hoạch của người khác, không mang ý nhờ vả ép buộc).
point: By the time + hiện tại đơn | By the time you arrive, I will have finished the report. Không dùng will sau by the time.
example: This time next year, I'll be working in Singapore. | Giờ này năm sau, tôi sẽ đang làm việc ở Singapore.
example: By 2040, most cars will have become electric. | Đến năm 2040, hầu hết ô tô sẽ đã chuyển sang chạy điện.
example: Don't call at eight – I'll be having a meeting. | Đừng gọi lúc tám giờ – lúc đó tôi đang họp.
example: By the time you get home, I will have cooked dinner. | Khi bạn về đến nhà, tôi sẽ nấu xong bữa tối.
mistake: By 2030, AI will replace many jobs. | By 2030, AI will have replaced many jobs. | by + mốc tương lai → tương lai hoàn thành.
mistake: By the time you will arrive, I'll have left. | By the time you arrive, I'll have left. | Sau by the time dùng hiện tại đơn.
mistake: This time tomorrow I will lie on the beach. | This time tomorrow I will be lying on the beach. | Đang diễn ra tại thời điểm tương lai → tiếp diễn.
ex: This time tomorrow, I ___ to London. | will fly ; will be flying ; will have flown | will be flying | This time tomorrow → tương lai tiếp diễn.
ex: By 2030, scientists ___ a cure for this disease. | will find ; will be finding ; will have found | will have found | By 2030 → tương lai hoàn thành.
ex: By the time you ___, the meeting will have started. | arrive ; will arrive ; arrived | arrive | Sau by the time dùng hiện tại đơn.
ex: At 10 p.m. tonight, she ___ her favourite show. | will be watching ; will have watched ; watches | will be watching | Thời điểm cụ thể trong tương lai → tiếp diễn.
ex: ___ you be using your laptop this evening? | Will ; Are ; Have | Will | Hỏi lịch sự: Will you be + V-ing?
ex: By next month, I ___ here for ten years. | will work ; will have worked ; will be work | will have worked | By next month → tương lai hoàn thành.
ex: By the end of the century, robots ___ most factory jobs. | will have taken over ; will be take over ; are taking over | will have taken over | By the end of... → tương lai hoàn thành.

## grammar
title: Câu điều kiện loại 3 & điều kiện hỗn hợp
summary: Câu điều kiện loại 3 nói về điều KHÔNG xảy ra trong quá khứ và kết quả tưởng tượng của nó – thường để diễn tả sự tiếc nuối. Câu điều kiện hỗn hợp kết nối giả định trong quá khứ với kết quả ở hiện tại (hoặc ngược lại).
formula: Loại 3: If + S + had + V3, S + would have + V3
formula: Hỗn hợp (quá khứ → hiện tại): If + S + had + V3, S + would + V (now)
formula: Hỗn hợp (hiện tại → quá khứ): If + S + V2, S + would have + V3
formula: Tiếc nuối: S + wish + S + had + V3 (I wish I had told the truth.)
point: Loại 3 = quá khứ không có thật | If I had known about the party, I would have come. (Thực tế: tôi không biết nên đã không đến.)
point: Hỗn hợp: quá khứ ảnh hưởng hiện tại | If she hadn't moved abroad, she would be married to him now. (Quá khứ: đã chuyển đi → hiện tại: không cưới.)
point: Hỗn hợp: hiện tại giải thích quá khứ | If he weren't so stubborn, he would have apologized yesterday. (Hiện tại: bướng → quá khứ: đã không xin lỗi.)
point: could have / might have | Thay would have bằng could have (đã có thể) hoặc might have (có lẽ đã): If we had talked, we could have avoided the conflict.
example: If you had told me the truth, I would have forgiven you. | Nếu bạn nói thật với tôi, tôi đã tha thứ cho bạn rồi.
example: If they hadn't argued, they would still be together. | Nếu họ không cãi nhau, giờ họ vẫn còn bên nhau.
example: I wish I hadn't said those words. | Tôi ước mình đã không nói những lời đó.
example: If she weren't so shy, she would have asked him out. | Nếu cô ấy không quá nhút nhát, cô ấy đã rủ anh ấy đi chơi rồi.
mistake: If I would have known, I would have helped. | If I had known, I would have helped. | Mệnh đề if dùng had + V3.
mistake: If I had studied, I would pass the exam last year. | If I had studied, I would have passed the exam last year. | Kết quả trong quá khứ → would have + V3.
mistake: I wish I didn't say that yesterday. | I wish I hadn't said that yesterday. | Tiếc về quá khứ → had + V3.
ex: If I ___ about the meeting, I would have come. | knew ; had known ; would know | had known | Loại 3: If + had + V3.
ex: If she had apologized, he ___ her. | would forgive ; would have forgiven ; forgave | would have forgiven | Loại 3: would have + V3.
ex: If we hadn't missed the flight, we ___ in Paris now. | would be ; would have been ; are | would be | Hỗn hợp: kết quả ở hiện tại (now).
ex: I wish I ___ him the truth. | told ; had told ; would tell | had told | Tiếc nuối quá khứ → had + V3.
ex: If he ___ so jealous, they wouldn't have broken up. | isn't ; weren't ; hadn't | weren't | Hỗn hợp: tính cách hiện tại → kết quả quá khứ.
ex: If you had listened to me, you ___ that mistake. | wouldn't make ; wouldn't have made ; didn't make | wouldn't have made | Loại 3.
ex: They ___ have reconciled if they had talked earlier. | could ; can ; will | could | could have + V3 = đã có thể.

## reading
title: The science of forgiveness
text: Holding on to anger can be exhausting. Psychologists have found that people who forgive tend to have lower stress levels and healthier relationships. Forgiving does not mean forgetting or pretending that nothing happened. It means choosing to let go of the desire for revenge. Interestingly, many people later realise that if they had communicated more openly, the conflict would never have started. Researchers suggest a simple first step: try to see the situation from the other person's perspective. Most misunderstandings are not caused by bad intentions, but by poor communication.
vi: Ôm giữ cơn giận có thể khiến ta kiệt sức. Các nhà tâm lý học phát hiện rằng những người biết tha thứ thường có mức độ căng thẳng thấp hơn và các mối quan hệ lành mạnh hơn. title: Rewilding: giving nature a second chance
text: Rewilding is a conservation approach that aims to restore ecosystems by letting nature take care of itself. Instead of managing every detail, scientists reintroduce species that have disappeared from an area, such as wolves or beavers, and then step back. One famous example is Yellowstone National Park, where wolves were brought back in 1995 after an absence of seventy years. The wolves, which hunted the deer, changed the behaviour of other animals, allowing trees and plants to grow again. Supporters believe rewilding is one of the most effective ways to protect biodiversity.
