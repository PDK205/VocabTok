## grammar
title: Từ nối chỉ sự tương phản (although, despite, however, whereas)
summary: Để lập luận chặt chẽ (rất quan trọng trong IELTS), bạn cần nối các ý trái ngược một cách chính xác. Mỗi từ nối đi với một cấu trúc ngữ pháp riêng.
formula: although / though / even though + mệnh đề (S + V)
formula: despite / in spite of + N / V-ing / the fact that + mệnh đề
formula: However, + mệnh đề (đứng đầu câu mới, sau dấu chấm hoặc chấm phẩy)
formula: whereas / while + mệnh đề (so sánh hai đối tượng trái ngược)
point: although vs despite | Although it rained, we went out. = Despite the rain, we went out. despite đi với danh từ / V-ing, KHÔNG đi trực tiếp với mệnh đề.
point: however | Là trạng từ nối hai câu: Online learning is flexible. However, it requires self-discipline. Không dùng however để nối hai mệnh đề chỉ bằng dấu phẩy.
point: whereas / while | So sánh hai sự vật tương phản: Public schools are free, whereas private schools charge high tuition fees.
point: Không dùng but cùng although | Although he studied hard, but he failed (sai) → Although he studied hard, he failed.
example: Although the course is expensive, it's worth it. | Mặc dù khóa học đắt, nó rất đáng giá.
example: Despite having little evidence, he won the debate. | Dù có ít bằng chứng, anh ấy vẫn thắng cuộc tranh luận.
example: Homework can be useful. However, too much of it causes stress. | Bài tập về nhà có thể hữu ích. Tuy nhiên, quá nhiều sẽ gây căng thẳng.
example: Some students prefer group work, whereas others learn better alone. | Một số học sinh thích làm nhóm, trong khi những em khác học tốt hơn một mình.
mistake: Despite it was difficult, she passed. | Although it was difficult, she passed. | despite không đi với mệnh đề.
mistake: Although he was tired, but he kept studying. | Although he was tired, he kept studying. | Không dùng but cùng although.
mistake: The exam was hard, however I passed. | The exam was hard. However, I passed. | however cần dấu chấm hoặc chấm phẩy phía trước.
ex: ___ the high tuition fees, many students choose private universities. | Although ; Despite ; However | Despite | Despite + danh từ.
ex: ___ she studied hard, she failed the exam. | Despite ; Although ; In spite of | Although | Although + mệnh đề.
ex: The course is useful. ___, it's very expensive. | Although ; However ; Despite | However | However đứng đầu câu mới.
ex: Public schools are free, ___ private ones are expensive. | whereas ; despite ; however | whereas | whereas so sánh hai đối tượng trái ngược.
ex: In spite of ___ tired, he finished the essay. | be ; being ; was | being | In spite of + V-ing.
ex: Despite the fact that he had no evidence, he ___ the debate. | won ; but won ; however won | won | Không thêm but sau despite.
ex: ___ online courses are flexible, they require discipline. | Despite ; Even though ; However | Even though | Even though + mệnh đề.

## grammar
title: Mệnh đề phân từ (Participle Clauses)
summary: Mệnh đề phân từ giúp rút gọn câu, nối hai hành động cùng chủ ngữ một cách tự nhiên và trang trọng. Thường gặp trong văn viết, truyện và bài thi IELTS.
formula: V-ing (chủ động, đồng thời / nguyên nhân): Walking through the old town, I saw many lanterns.
formula: V3 (bị động): Built in the 11th century, the temple is very old.
formula: Having + V3 (hành động xảy ra trước): Having visited Hue, we flew to Hoi An.
formula: Chủ ngữ của hai mệnh đề phải giống nhau
point: V-ing: chủ động | Thay cho mệnh đề có cùng chủ ngữ mang nghĩa chủ động: When I walked along the river → Walking along the river, I took many photos. Cũng diễn tả nguyên nhân: Feeling tired, she went to bed early.
point: V3: bị động | Founded in 1070, the Temple of Literature is Vietnam's first university. (= It was founded in 1070.)
point: Having + V3: hành động trước | Nhấn mạnh hành động thứ nhất đã hoàn tất: Having finished the tour, we had lunch.
point: Lỗi "treo" chủ ngữ | Walking into the museum, the paintings amazed me (sai – bức tranh không đi bộ) → Walking into the museum, I was amazed by the paintings.
example: Located in the north, Sapa is famous for its rice terraces. | Nằm ở phía Bắc, Sa Pa nổi tiếng với ruộng bậc thang.
example: Wearing traditional clothes, the dancers performed beautifully. | Mặc trang phục truyền thống, các vũ công biểu diễn rất đẹp.
example: Having lived in Japan for years, she understands the culture well. | Đã sống ở Nhật nhiều năm, cô ấy hiểu rõ văn hóa nơi đây.
example: Made by hand, these ceramics are very expensive. | Được làm thủ công, những món gốm này rất đắt.
mistake: Building in 1802, the citadel is huge. | Built in 1802, the citadel is huge. | Thành được xây → bị động → V3.
mistake: Arriving at the temple, the gate was closed. | Arriving at the temple, we found the gate closed. | Chủ ngữ hai vế phải giống nhau.
mistake: Having visit the museum, we went home. | Having visited the museum, we went home. | Having + V3.

ex: ___ by hand, these lanterns are unique. | Making ; Made ; Make | Made | Đèn lồng được làm → V3.
ex: ___ tired after the trip, she slept for twelve hours. | Felt ; Feeling ; Having felt | Feeling | Nguyên nhân, chủ động → V-ing.
ex: ___ in Hoi An, the old house is now a museum. | Locating ; Located ; Locate | Located | be located in = nằm ở.
ex: ___ the local language, he easily made friends. | Spoken ; Speaking ; Speaks | Speaking | Chủ động → V-ing.

summary: Thay vì lặp lại said, người nói ở trình độ cao dùng động từ tường thuật chính xác hơn để truyền tải ý định: khuyên, cảnh báo, thừa nhận, phủ nhận... Mỗi động từ đi với một cấu trúc riêng.
formula: V + to V: agree, refuse, promise, offer, threaten (He refused to pay.)
formula: V + O + to V: advise, warn, persuade, encourage, remind, tell (She advised me to read the reviews.)
formula: V + V-ing: deny, admit, suggest, recommend (He denied lying.)
formula: V + that + mệnh đề: suggest, recommend, admit, claim, insist (They claimed that the product was natural.)
formula: V + O + of / for + V-ing: accuse sb of, blame sb for (They accused the brand of lying.)
point: suggest không đi với O + to V | She suggested going (đúng) / She suggested that we (should) go (đúng) / She suggested us to go (SAI).
point: Phủ định với to V | warn / advise / tell sb NOT to V: The doctor warned me not to eat too much sugar.
point: Chọn động từ theo ý định | "I'll pay for it" → He offered to pay. "I didn't steal it!" → He denied stealing it. "It was me" → She admitted breaking it.
point: Lùi thì sau that | Động từ tường thuật ở quá khứ thì mệnh đề that thường lùi thì: The company claimed that its product was eco-friendly.
example: The influencer admitted being paid for the review. | Người có ảnh hưởng thừa nhận đã được trả tiền cho bài đánh giá.
example: My friend advised me not to trust online ads. | Bạn tôi khuyên tôi đừng tin quảng cáo trên mạng.
example: The company denied using fake reviews. | Công ty phủ nhận việc dùng đánh giá giả.
example: Experts recommend reading the small print carefully. | Các chuyên gia khuyên nên đọc kỹ điều khoản.
mistake: He suggested me to buy it. | He suggested that I buy it. | suggest không đi với O + to V.
mistake: She denied to take the money. | She denied taking the money. | deny + V-ing.
mistake: They warned us don't click the link. | They warned us not to click the link. | warn sb not to V.
ex: "I didn't lie!" → He denied ___. | to lie ; lying ; lie | lying | deny + V-ing.
ex: "You should compare prices." → She advised me ___ prices. | compare ; to compare ; comparing | to compare | advise sb to V.
ex: "Let's watch the ad again." → He suggested ___ the ad again. | watching ; to watch ; watch | watching | suggest + V-ing.
ex: "I'll give you a discount." → The seller offered ___ me a discount. | giving ; to give ; give | to give | offer + to V.
ex: "Don't believe that ad." → My mum warned me ___ that ad. | not believe ; not to believe ; to not believing | not to believe | warn sb not to V.
ex: "Yes, we used fake reviews." → The company admitted ___ fake reviews. | using ; to use ; use | using | admit + V-ing.
ex: They accused the brand ___ misleading customers. | for ; of ; with | of | accuse sb of + V-ing.

title: Bị động với động từ khuyết thiếu & thì hoàn thành
summary: Trong tin tức về tội phạm và pháp luật, câu bị động xuất hiện liên tục vì ta quan tâm đến nạn nhân, nghi phạm và hành động hơn là người thực hiện. Ở trình độ B2 cần thành thạo bị động với modal và thì hoàn thành.
formula: Modal + be + V3: Criminals should be punished.
formula: Modal + have been + V3 (quá khứ): The thief must have been seen by someone.
formula: have / has been + V3: The suspect has been arrested.
formula: had been + V3: The money had been stolen before the police arrived.
formula: being + V3 (sau giới từ / động từ): He was afraid of being caught.
point: Bị động với modal | can / could / should / must / might / will + be + V3: This law must be changed. Online fraud can be prevented.
point: Modal hoàn thành bị động | Suy đoán về quá khứ ở dạng bị động: The door can't have been locked – there's no damage.
point: Bị động hoàn thành | Nhấn mạnh kết quả: Three men have been charged with theft. / By the time we got home, our house had been broken into.
point: being + V3 | Dùng sau giới từ hoặc động từ cần V-ing: She hates being watched. / He denied being involved.
example: Shoplifters will be prosecuted. | Kẻ trộm đồ trong cửa hàng sẽ bị truy tố.

example: Nobody likes being treated unfairly. | Không ai thích bị đối xử bất công.

mistake: Two men have arrested. | Two men have been arrested. | Bị động hoàn thành: have been + V3.
mistake: He was afraid of catching by the police. | He was afraid of being caught by the police. | Nghĩa bị động sau giới từ: being + V3.

title: Accidental discoveries
text: Some of the most important scientific breakthroughs happened by accident. In 1928, Alexander Fleming returned from holiday to find that mould had grown on one of his experiments. Instead of throwing it away, he looked into it more closely and found out that the mould killed bacteria. This discovery led to penicillin, which has saved millions of lives. Similarly, the microwave oven was invented after an engineer noticed that a chocolate bar in his pocket had melted while he was working with radar. These stories show that great discoveries require not only luck, but also curiosity and careful observation.
